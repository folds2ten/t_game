Controls & spells

Move WASD / arrows, QEZC diagonals, or click ground.
Attack click a creature, Space = nearest, Tab = cycle.
Talk Enter, then type. NPCs answer to hi, keywords and bye.
Loot click a corpse next to you. Hotkeys 1–9, right-click one to change it.
